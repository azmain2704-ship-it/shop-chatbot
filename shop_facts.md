# Bayside Bakes: shop facts

(A made-up bakery used for this demo. Swap this file for a real business's details.)

## About
Bayside Bakes is a small independent bakery and coffee shop in Cardiff Bay.
Everything is baked on site each morning.

## Address and contact
- Address: 14 Mermaid Quay, Cardiff Bay, Cardiff CF10 5BZ
- Phone: 029 2000 1234
- Email: hello@baysidebakes.example
- Nearest station: Cardiff Bay (5 minutes' walk)

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 7:30am to 5:00pm
- Saturday: 8:00am to 4:00pm
- Sunday: 9:00am to 2:00pm
- Closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

## Menu and prices
### Savoury
- Sausage roll: £1.60
- Vegan sausage roll: £1.70
- Cheese and onion pasty: £2.10
- Welsh rarebit toastie: £4.50
- Soup of the day with bread: £4.20

### Sweet
- Welsh cakes (each): £0.90
- Bara brith slice: £2.20
- Chocolate brownie: £2.50
- Cinnamon swirl: £2.30

### Drinks
- Filter coffee: £1.80
- Latte or cappuccino: £2.90
- Tea: £1.50
- Hot chocolate: £2.80
- Oat, soy or almond milk: free

### Meal deal
- Any savoury item plus any regular hot drink: £4.00 (before 11am only)

## Ordering and payment
- Card and contactless accepted. No cash over £50 notes.
- Click and collect: order by phone at least 1 hour ahead.
- No delivery service.
- Celebration cakes: order by phone at least 3 days ahead, from £25.

## Seating and access
- 20 seats inside, 8 outside (weather permitting).
- Step-free entrance and an accessible toilet.
- Dogs welcome in the outdoor seating area only.
- Free Wi-Fi for customers.

## Dietary information
- Vegan options: vegan sausage roll, soup of the day (ask staff which soup is on), plant milks.
- Allergen information changes daily. Customers with allergies must speak to a member of staff before ordering.
